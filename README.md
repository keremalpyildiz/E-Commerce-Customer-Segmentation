# E-Commerce Customer Segmentation using Machine Learning 🎯

## Project Overview
This project applies Unsupervised Machine Learning (K-Means Clustering) to segment e-commerce customers based on their Annual Income and Spending Score. The goal is to transform raw customer data into actionable marketing strategies, enabling personalized offers and maximizing business profitability.

## Technologies Used
* **Python** (Data Analysis)
* **Pandas** (Data Manipulation)
* **Scikit-Learn** (K-Means Algorithm)
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** (Data Visualization)

## Business Value & Marketing Strategy
By clustering the customers into 5 distinct segments, the business can apply targeted marketing campaigns instead of blind advertising:

* **Target 1: The "Sleepers" (High Income, Low Spending):** The most critical group. They have the budget but aren't buying. **Strategy:** Send aggressive, personalized discounts and limited-time offers to win them over.
* **Target 2: "Loyal VIPs" (High Income, High Spending):** The core revenue generators. **Strategy:** No need for high discounts. Offer them early access to new collections and premium loyalty programs.
* **Target 3: "Bargain Hunters" (Low Income, High Spending):** **Strategy:** Target them with affordable, fast-moving consumer goods and flash sales.
* **Target 4: "Cold Audience" (Low Income, Low Spending):** **Strategy:** Ignore them to save and optimize the advertising budget.
* **Target 5: "Standard Majority" (Average Income & Spending):** **Strategy:** Keep them engaged with standard newsletters and seasonal campaigns.

## Conclusion
This model allows an e-commerce platform to shift from a "one-size-fits-all" approach to a highly optimized, AI-driven marketing framework.
